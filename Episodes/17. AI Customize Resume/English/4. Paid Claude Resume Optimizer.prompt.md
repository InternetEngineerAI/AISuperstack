## PHASE 2 — JOB DESCRIPTION INTELLIGENCE

Analyze the job posting and extract:

**Explicit Keywords**
- Hard skills, tools, technologies, certifications mentioned directly
- For every keyword capture BOTH the full term AND its acronym
  Example: "Search Engine Optimization (SEO)",
  "Applicant Tracking System (ATS)",
  "Customer Relationship Management (CRM)"
- Covering both forms doubles ATS match probability

**Implicit Keywords**
- Skills the role assumes but doesn't directly state
- Infer from responsibilities what tools/processes are standard
  for this seniority level
- Cross-reference with competitor postings found in Phase 1

**Seniority Signals**
- IC roles: "execute," "build," "deliver," "manage"
- Lead/Manager: "own," "drive," "oversee," "partner with"
- Director+: "define," "lead strategy," "scale," "transform"

**Priority Stack**
- Rank the top 5 responsibilities by emphasis
  (frequency + placement + specificity)
- These become the organizing principle for bullet reordering

| Keyword | Category | Priority (High/Medium/Low) |

## PHASE 7 — GAP BRIDGING

Identify ALL resume risks and assign each a resolution action:

**Skill Gaps — assign one of:**
1. REFRAME — candidate has this but it's not framed correctly
2. SURFACE — candidate likely has this but didn't include it — ask them
3. BRIDGE — candidate lacks this directly; write the transferable framing
4. FLAG — genuine gap; candidate should address in interviews

**Timeline & Career Risks — assign one of:**
5. GAP — employment gap of 3+ months
   → Reframe as: consulting, certifications, caregiving,
     entrepreneurship, or professional development as applicable
6. SHORT TENURE — role lasting less than 18 months
   → Reframe context: acquisition, layoff, contract role,
     org restructure — whatever is accurate
7. CAREER PIVOT — transition between industries or functions
   → Build a transferable skills bridge that makes the pivot
     feel intentional and strategic, not accidental
8. OVERQUALIFIED — candidate appears too senior for the role
   → Reframe scope language downward without diminishing impact

For each risk write the specific alternative phrasing to use.

Return this table:

| Risk Type | Description | Action | Suggested Reframe |
|-----------|-------------|--------|------------------|

## PHASE 8 — QUANTIFICATION ENGINE

Convert every vague achievement into a metrics-driven outcome.

**Formula:** High-signal verb + Scope/Context + Quantified Outcome + Timeframe

**Transformation examples:**
- "Responsible for team performance"
  → "Led 5-person team delivering weekly reports with 100% on-time
     rate over 18 months"
- "Helped with client onboarding"
  → "Onboarded 23 enterprise clients across Q3–Q4, reducing average
     time-to-value from 45 to 28 days"
- "Managed social media accounts"
  → "Grew LinkedIn following 340% in 6 months through data-driven
     content cadence, generating 12 inbound leads"
- "Responsible for sales"
  → "Increased pipeline revenue by X% through [specific action]"
- "Improved operations"
  → "Reduced processing time by X% by implementing [specific change]"

**Placeholder protocol:**
- Where metrics are missing insert: [INSERT: team size / % / revenue / timeframe]
- Never fabricate — flag every gap for the candidate to fill

**Verb bank by seniority:**
- IC: Built, Engineered, Executed, Delivered, Automated, Reduced,
      Increased, Resolved, Designed, Implemented
- Lead: Led, Coached, Streamlined, Launched, Owned, Drove,
        Standardized, Mentored, Coordinated
- Director+: Defined, Transformed, Scaled, Restructured, Pioneered,
             Secured, Aligned, Spearheaded, Established
