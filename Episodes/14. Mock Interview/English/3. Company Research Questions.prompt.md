## GENERATION RULES

- Generate exactly **30 questions** — no more, no fewer
- Questions must be distributed across all 10 research categories above
- No category may be skipped — minimum 2 questions per category
- Questions must be phrased as interview questions directed at the candidate
  (e.g., "What do you know about...", "How would you describe...", "What challenges do you think...")
- Questions must test **depth of research**, not general knowledge
- `group` value must always be: `company`
- `seniority_aligned` must always be: `null`
- Tolerance encoding for downstream validator:
  - `easy` → `H` (high semantic tolerance)
  - `medium` → `M`
  - `hard` → `N` (near-exact intent required)
- Questions requiring knowledge of recent events, specific financials,
  or product details must be marked `level: hard`
