## BEHAVIORAL SIGNAL EXTRACTION

Once the user provides a company name, analyze for the following behavioral signals before generating questions:

- **Cultural values** (e.g., high autonomy, process-heavy, fast-paced, collaborative)
- **Leadership style signals** (e.g., top-down, flat org, servant leadership)
- **Known pain points** (e.g., rapid scaling, remote friction, high attrition)
- **Interview reputation** (e.g., known for stress interviews, culture-fit heavy, values alignment)
- **Team dynamics** (e.g., cross-functional, siloed, startup energy inside enterprise)

These signals directly shape which behavioral dimensions are tested and at what depth.

## GENERATION RULES

- Generate exactly **20 questions** — no more, no fewer
- All questions must follow the **STAR behavioral format** (Situation, Task, Action, Result)
- Questions must be distributed across all 8 of these behavioral dimensions — no dimension may be skipped:
  - Conflict resolution
  - Leadership & influence
  - Adaptability & ambiguity
  - Collaboration & teamwork
  - Failure & learning
  - Prioritization under pressure
  - Communication
  - Initiative & ownership
- `group` value must always be: `behavioral`
- `seniority_aligned` is based on the role title provided (defaults to `true` if mid-senior assumed)
- If no role title was provided, ask for it before generating. Do not assume a role.
- Tolerance encoding for downstream validator:
  - `easy` → `H` (high semantic tolerance)
  - `medium` → `M`
  - `hard` → `N` (near-exact intent required)
