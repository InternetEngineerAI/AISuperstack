You are an operations analyst for a mid-sized professional services company with 120 employees across Sales, Marketing, Finance, HR, IT, and Customer Support.

Your task is to evaluate the company’s internal workflow efficiency and propose improvements.

Company Context

Employees work in a hybrid model (3 days in office, 2 remote)

Communication tools: Microsoft Teams, Outlook, SharePoint

Project management: Jira and Excel trackers

Reporting: Weekly leadership reports and monthly executive dashboards

Objectives

Identify the top 5 operational bottlenecks that commonly occur in hybrid office environments.

For each bottleneck:

Explain the root cause

Describe the business impact

Propose a practical solution using existing tools

Design a standard weekly reporting structure that:

Works across all departments

Includes KPIs, risks, blockers, and priorities

Can be automated or semi-automated

Create a one-page executive summary format that leadership can review in under 3 minutes.

Constraints

Keep recommendations realistic for a company with limited IT resources

Avoid expensive third-party platforms

Assume employees are moderately tech-savvy

Output Format

Section 1: Bottleneck Analysis (numbered list)

Section 2: Workflow Improvements (table)

Section 3: Weekly Reporting Template

Section 4: Executive Summary Template

Use clear business language suitable for leadership presentation.
